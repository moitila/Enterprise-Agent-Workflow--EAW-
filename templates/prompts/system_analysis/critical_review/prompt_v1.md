# Critical review — system analysis

Compare the source inventory and every system baseline, topology, and authority claim against its cited evidence. Identify omissions, over-compression, unsupported inference, contradiction, ambiguity, unresolved/TBD items, and questions belonging to downstream work. Be specific: cite source and locator, explain impact, and propose a correction only when evidence supports it. Uncertainty is analysis content, not a reason to wait for approval. Write `analysis/40_critical_review.md` and the compact completed handoff required by the phase.

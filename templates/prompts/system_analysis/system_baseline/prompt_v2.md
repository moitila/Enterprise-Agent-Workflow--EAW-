Analyze the system evidenced by declared sources.

Explain purpose, scope, actors, capabilities, decisions, constraints, and open questions with source traceability.
## INPUT
Use source inventory and all declared evidence.
## OUTPUT
Write `analysis/10_system_analysis.candidate.md`, `analysis/11_decision_register.yaml`, `analysis/12_open_questions.md`, and compact completed `investigations/20_handoff.json`.
## OUTPUT_STRUCTURE
Separate observed facts, interpretations, decisions, and unknowns; provide locators and rationale.
## READ_SCOPE
Card context, source inventory, and declared sources.
## WRITE_SCOPE
Only the required outputs above.
## RULES
Preserve nuance, conflicts, and TBDs; do not compress away material distinctions.
## FORBIDDEN
No approval/publication steps, invented authority, or fixture-specific assumptions.
## FAIL_CONDITIONS
Missing required output only; unknowns remain documented and do not block progression.

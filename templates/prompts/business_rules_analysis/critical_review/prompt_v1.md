# Critical review — business rules

Compare each rule and relationship with the declared upstream analyses and source locators. Identify missing rules, superficial wording, over-compression, unsupported inference, contradictions, ambiguity, preserved upstream TBD states, and questions for downstream implementation. Never silently resolve conflicts. Record evidence and impact in `analysis/30_critical_review.md`, plus the compact completed handoff required by the phase. Uncertainty is part of the analysis and does not require approval to continue.

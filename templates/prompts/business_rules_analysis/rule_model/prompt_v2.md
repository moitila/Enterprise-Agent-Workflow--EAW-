# Business rule model

## ROLE
Extract traceable business rules from upstream evidence.
## OBJECTIVE
Describe literal rule meaning, actors, conditions, outcomes, exceptions, status, and open questions.
## INPUT
Use all declared upstream analyses and source inventory.
## OUTPUT
Write phase-declared narrative, structured catalog, questions, and handoff.
## OUTPUT_STRUCTURE
Each rule has evidence locators, source wording, interpretation, upstream status, and uncertainty.
## READ_SCOPE
Declared sources only.
## WRITE_SCOPE
Phase-required outputs.
## RULES
Do not silently normalize conflicts or infer missing rules.
## FORBIDDEN
No approval/publication/consumption gate.
## FAIL_CONDITIONS
Missing required output only; unresolved rule status is retained.
